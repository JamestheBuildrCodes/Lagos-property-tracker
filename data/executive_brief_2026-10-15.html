Recommended actions

  1. Treat previously observed URLs missing this week as 'not observed'; verify status directly before calling any property sold or delisted.
  2. Compare like-for-like listings by location, transaction type, bedroom count, size and source update date.
  3. Click the original listing for every high-value or unusual figure. Confirm the price, status, fees, title and exact location with the advertiser.
  4. Use the Area Scorecard only where sample counts are adequate; commission additional checks where the sample is thin.
