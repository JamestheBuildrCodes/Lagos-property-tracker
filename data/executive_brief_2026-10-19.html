What changed this week?

  • Lekki Phase 1 has the most robust sample (100 listings), with a median 3BR sale price of ₦420M and a gross rent yield proxy of 4.05%.
  • Victoria Island shows an anomalous gross rent yield proxy of 49.23%, likely due to data inconsistencies or extreme outliers, as the median rent (₦320M) is disproportionately high relative to the median sale price (₦650M).
  • Asokoro offers the highest reliable gross rent yield proxy at 10.0%, with a median 3BR sale price of ₦200M and median rent of ₦20M.
  • Sale price asking prices decreased significantly in Old Ikoyi (-30%), Lekki Phase 1 (-23.6%), and Ikeja GRA (-27.3%) over the 40-day period.
  • Land prices in Lekki Phase 1 dropped by 49.2% and in Asokoro by 40.8% over the same period, while Victoria Island land prices increased by 100.6%.

What this means for the business

  • This scan retained 307 listings that passed the current source and location checks across 9 monitored locations.
  • The tracked sample shows apartment sale asking prices decreased 6.7% in the tracked sample; annual rent asking prices was unchanged 0.0% in the tracked sample; land asking price per square metre decreased 30.6% in the tracked sample. These movements are sample-level signals, not market-wide changes.
  • No price reductions were verified in the comparison file. Do not infer that sellers are not negotiating; the scan may not have captured a price change.
  • 214 previous URLs were not observed in this scan. This does not prove that the properties sold or were delisted; it may also reflect coverage or extraction differences.
  • Sample caution: the thinnest covered locations are Maitama, Ikeja GRA, Banana Island. Treat these as watchlist observations, not reliable area-wide benchmarks.
  • Listing-card cross-check: 307 rows passed deterministic source-evidence checks; 33 rows were excluded from comparable statistics for review. This check tests consistency with captured public listing text; it does not prove ownership or a closed transaction.
  • Rental screening: Victoria Island has an indicative gross asking-rent yield proxy of 49.23%. It is a screening ratio, not net return, and should be relied on only with a sufficiently large and comparable sample.
  • Buyer screening: Lekki Phase 1 is the current lowest observed sale-price-per-square-metre screen among qualifying locations (sale sample: 40). Check that property type, condition, size and tenure are comparable before interpreting this as value.
  • Land screening: Asokoro has the lowest observed land asking price per sqm in the current sample. This is a lead for due diligence, not a recommendation to buy.

    • Business implications

    • Investors seeking rental income may find better gross yields in Asokoro (10.0%) and Ikeja GRA (7.5%) compared to Lekki Phase 1 (4.05%), though net yields will be lower after expenses.
    • The significant drop in asking prices for sales in Old Ikoyi, Lekki Phase 1, and Ikeja GRA may present negotiation opportunities for buyers, but this is not confirmed by transaction data.
    • The extreme volatility in land prices (e.g., -49.2% in Lekki Phase 1, +100.6% in Victoria Island) suggests high uncertainty in the land market, requiring careful due diligence.
    • The lack of data for Eko Atlantic and Wuse limits strategic planning for these areas, and the thin sample in Maitama makes it a high-risk area for data-driven decisions.

Recommended actions

  1. Conduct on-the-ground verification of asking prices in Old Ikoyi, Lekki Phase 1, and Ikeja GRA to assess if the observed price reductions reflect actual market softening or seller strategy.
  2. Investigate the data anomaly in Victoria Island's rent yield proxy by reviewing individual listings to identify outliers or data entry errors.
  3. Expand data collection to include Eko Atlantic and Wuse to fill critical gaps in market coverage.
  4. Increase sample size for Maitama by aggregating data over a longer period or adding more sources to improve reliability.
  5. Monitor land price trends closely, especially in Lekki Phase 1 and Victoria Island, given the extreme volatility observed.
